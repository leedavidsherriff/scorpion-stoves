Chimney repointing

When the mortar goes, the water comes in.

A stack sits in the worst of the weather. Once the joints wear away, rain gets into the brick and tracks down inside, and the damp patch on a bedroom ceiling usually started up here years earlier.

  • A damp patch on the chimney breast or the ceiling below
  • Sand or bits of mortar in the gutter
  • Grass or a weed growing out of the stack
  • A pot sitting crooked, or cracked flaunching

The ones we get asked most.

How often does a chimney need sweeping?

Once a year for most people. If you burn wood hard all winter, twice a year is safer: once before the season and once part way through.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, every sweep comes with one. Home insurers often ask for it, and most letting agreements need one each year.

Will it make a mess of my front room?

No. Sheets go down, the fireplace is sealed and everything comes out through a vacuum.

How long does it take?

About 45 minutes for a straightforward sweep. A smoke test adds another 15 or so.

Do you sweep for landlords and letting agents?

Yes, and the certificate can go straight to whoever needs it for the file.
