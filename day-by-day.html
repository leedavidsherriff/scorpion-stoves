A knockout, day by day

Sheets down on day one. Fire lit at the end.

A full fireplace knockout takes two to three days. Gareth tells you how long your job will take on the survey.

Day one The mucky bit

  1. 8amDust sheets everywhere. Furniture moved, the room sealed off.
  2. MorningThe old fireplace is knocked out and the opening checked.
  3. AfternoonCement boarded, and plastered where it’s needed.

Day two or three The good bit

  1. MorningThe slate hearth goes down and the stove is set in place.
  2. AfternoonEverything connected and checked, ready to be signed off with building regs.

First burn done with you. Gareth lights it, shows you the air controls, and leaves you with a warm room and no mess.
