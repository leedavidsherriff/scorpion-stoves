How it works

Four steps to a lit fire.

A free look at your room, a price in writing, the job done and signed off with building regs, and the first fire lit with you. That’s it.

Book a free surveyOr order online

GarethNo salesman, no pushy follow-ups. Just me, a tape measure and a cuppa.

Free survey

Gareth comes out and checks your chimney, flue and hearth clearances, and works out which of his five jobs yours is. No obligation.

Your price in writing

The guide prices on this site are for a typical room. After the survey you get one confirmed price, in writing, before any work starts. If anything about your chimney changes the job, you hear it then, not halfway through.

Fitted and signed off

Dust sheets down first thing. A full fireplace knockout takes two to three days; Gareth tells you how long yours will take on the survey. Every installation is signed off with building regs.

First burn, done with you

Gareth lights the first fire with you, shows you the air controls and what to burn, and leaves you with the building regs sign-off for your records.

The jobs Gareth does

Five jobs, five prices.

  • Full fireplace knockoutThe old fireplace knocked out and the whole opening rebuilt round a new stove. Stove included.
  • Stove fitted, chimney stack in placeYour fireplace is ready and your chimney stack is still up. Gareth fits the stove. Stove is an extra cost.
  • Stove fitted, chimney stack removedYour chimney stack has been taken down. Gareth fits the stove and its flue. Stove is an extra cost.
  • Twin-wall flue up the side of the houseNo chimney at all. A black twin-wall flue goes out through the wall and up the side of the house. Stove included.
  • Single-storey extension, flue through the roofA stove in a single-storey extension, with an insulated flue straight up through the roof. Stove included.
