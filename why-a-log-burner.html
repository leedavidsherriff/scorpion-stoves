Why a log burner

Heat the room you live in, not the whole house.

We won’t invent a savings figure for you, because every house is different. But there are three honest reasons people get one.

1 room

Zone your evenings

Most families live in one room after tea. A stove lets you turn the thermostat down and heat that room properly, instead of warming empty bedrooms.

75%+

Modern stoves waste very little

A modern Ecodesign stove turns around three-quarters or more of the wood into heat in the room. An open fire manages nearer a fifth; the rest goes up the chimney.

0 plugs

It works when nothing else does

A wood burner doesn’t care about power cuts or a boiler that’s given up. Dry logs in the store, warm house.
